## The problem Bindbee solves

Your customers run their people data on BambooHR, Workday, ADP, Gusto, UKG, Rippling, and dozens of others. Each one has a different API, a different auth model, a different pagination scheme, and a different idea of what an "employee" is.

Building and maintaining those integrations yourself is not one project. It is a permanent team.

Bindbee gives you **one API, one auth model, and one set of unified data models** across every supported system.

```http
GET https://api.bindbee.dev/api/hris/v1/employees
```

That request returns the same `HrisEmployee` shape whether your customer is on BambooHR or Workday.

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your customer connects their system">
    You send them a [Magic Link](/features/magic-link), or embed the connection
    flow directly in your product with [Bindbee Embed](/sdk/bindbee-embed).
    They authorize once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bindbee syncs and normalizes">
    Bindbee pulls data from the third-party system on a schedule, maps it into
    unified models, and stores the normalized result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You read from one API">
    You call Bindbee's unified endpoints with a `connector_token` that identifies
    that customer's connection. No per-vendor code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You stay in sync">
    [Webhooks](/webhooks/overview) tell you when data changes so you don't have
    to poll.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Bindbee reads from a **normalized copy** of your customer's data, not live from
  the third-party API on every request. This is what makes response times and
  rate limits predictable — and it is also why understanding
  [how syncing works](/explanation/how-syncing-works) matters.
</Info>

## Product categories

| Category | What it covers | Base path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HRIS** | HR, payroll, directory, benefits, time & attendance | `/api/hris/v1/` |
| **ATS** | Recruiting — candidates, jobs, applications, offers | `/api/ats/v1/` |
| **LMS** | Learning — users, courses, content, enrollments, completions | `/api/lms/v1/` |

Platform endpoints that are not category-specific — connectors, custom fields, passthrough, lookups — live under `/api/v1/` and `/api/embedded/v1/`.
