Pull the employee list a carrier needs to quote: who is eligible, their date of birth, their location, and their dependents.

## What you'll use

**Models** - [employee](/hris/employee/get-employees), [employment](/hris/employments/get-employments), [dependent](/hris/dependents/get-dependents), [location](/hris/locations/get-locations), [company](/hris/companies/get-companies)

**Events** - `connector_synced`

<Info>
  **Before you start**

  - You have the carrier's required field list. Changing scope later means a resync.
  - You've agreed with the customer which employees are being quoted. No filter means "eligible".
</Info>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scope to what a census needs">
    Under **Configure → Scoping**, exclude national ID and bank info - a quote never needs them. Keep date of birth, home postcode and location, because carriers rate on those.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read only after a completed sync">
    Gate the extract on `connector_synced`, not a timer - see [Trigger a job on completion](/guides/reading-writing/syncing). A census read mid-sync is quietly short.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fetch the population">
    ```bash
    curl --request GET \
      --url 'https://api.bindbee.dev/api/hris/v1/employees?employment_status=ACTIVE&page_size=200&expand=work_locations' \
      --header 'Authorization: Bearer <BINDBEE_API_KEY>' \
      --header 'X-Connector-Token: <CONNECTOR_TOKEN>'
    ```

    Filters as per data you required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Page to the end">
    Follow the cursor until it runs out - see [Pagination](/api-reference/basics/pagination). A census that stops at page one looks like a success.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add dependents">
    Read dependent benefits for who is actually covered, and dependents for their dates of birth. Counting dependent records instead of coverage inflates the census - see [Benefits](/guides/data-models/benefits).
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The headcount doesn't match the customer's">
    Check that your [filters](/guides/reading-writing/reading-data/filters) are the right ones for the population you want, that you paged to the end so the full set loaded - see [Pagination](/api-reference/basics/pagination) - and that the sync had finished before you read.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Missing fields or data?">
    Verify whether [scoping](/get-started/scoping) is enabled. If it is, then check the permissions for the customer integration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
