---
title: "Connectors"
description: "A connector is one customer's authorized link to one third-party system - where you find its status, issues, and logs."
---

A connector is one customer's authorized link to one third-party system. Each one is specific to a single integration and carries the credentials and state needed to move data between the two - one connector per customer, per system.

Find them under **Connectors** in the Dashboard, with separate **Development** and **Production** tabs.

<img
  src="https://images.unifyx.dev/documentation_images/connector.png"
  alt="The Connectors list in the Bindbee Dashboard"
  className="block"
/>

<Tip>
  From a connector you can also open its [Issues](/platform/issues) and its
  [Logs](/platform/logs) - both are the fastest route to why a sync misbehaved.
</Tip>

See [Core concepts](/concepts/core-concepts) for how connectors relate to environments, syncs, and connector tokens.
