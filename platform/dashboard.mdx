---
title: "Dashboard"
description: "The central place to see how your integrations are performing: API volume, connector health, and which customer is on which system."
---

The Dashboard is where you manage your Bindbee integrations from one place, and it is what you land on after signing in. It gives you an overview of how they are performing: how many API requests have been made, which of your customers is on which connector, and whether any connector currently has a problem.

From here you can also reach [Logs](/platform/logs), the list of open [Issues](/platform/issues), and every [connector](/platform/connectors) you have created.
