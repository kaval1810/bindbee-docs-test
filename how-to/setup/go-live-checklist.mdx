title: "Go-Live Checklist"
description: "Everything to verify before pointing production traffic at Bindbee. Grouped by failure mode, with the reason each item is on the list."
---

Work through this before your first real customer connects. Most items exist because skipping them produces a failure that is silent rather than loud.

---

## Credentials & environment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Production API key is in a secret manager">
    Not in source, not in a `.env` committed to the repo, not in CI logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No Development keys anywhere in the production path">
    They cannot read Production data and fail confusingly if they leak in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connector tokens are encrypted at rest">
    You are storing one credential per customer. Treat the table accordingly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credentials are redacted from logs and error tracking">
    Check your HTTP client's request logging and your error tracker's request
    capture - both commonly record headers by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Key rotation is a runbook, not a discovery">
    Regenerating a Production key invalidates the old one **immediately**. Know
    every service that holds it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Region is consistent">
    Backend base URL, SDK `serverUrl`, and token exchange all point at the same
    region.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connection flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="origin_id is your stable internal tenant ID">
    Not a name, not an email. This is your only reliable join key back to your
    own database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Token exchange happens server-side">
    `temporary_token` → `connector_token` requires the API key. It must never run
    in the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You store connector_id and integration_slug, not just the token">
    You will need both for support and for feature-flagging.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Abandoned connections are handled">
    `INCOMPLETE` connectors are normal. Give the customer a way to resume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-linking is implemented">
    `RELINK_NEEDED` is unrecoverable without the customer. Ship the reconnect
    path before you need it - it preserves the existing connector and token.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pagination terminates on cursor == null">
    Not on a short page. A short page is not the last page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filters are carried on every page">
    Dropping them mid-pagination gives undefined results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Incremental reads use a per-connector watermark">
    Captured before the run, saved only on success.
  </Step>
  <Step title="All writes to your store are idempotent upserts on id">
    You will receive the same record repeatedly. See
    [Field semantics](/concepts/enum-values).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every field is treated as nullable">
    Any relation or attribute can be null on some connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No exhaustive switch over an enum without a default">
    Unmapped vendor values pass through verbatim. Defaulting an unknown
    employment status to "inactive" can deprovision a real employee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="include_raw_data is off in bulk pipelines">
    It pulls sensitive fields the unified model deliberately omits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Writing data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request bodies are built from the Meta schema">
    Not hardcoded. Requirements differ per integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="integration_params and additional_attributes are used correctly">
    A required integration parameter placed in `additional_attributes` fails
    silently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="501 Not Implemented is handled as its own branch">
    It means the schema is unpublished, not that the write is unsupported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes are never blind-retried">
    They are not idempotent. A retried timeout can duplicate a record in your
    customer's HRIS. Record a request key; resolve unknowns by reading back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The UI doesn't block on readback">
    A 2xx means the source system accepted it - not that Bindbee can return it
    yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Webhooks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Signatures are verified on every request">
    Against raw bytes, with a constant-time comparison. Fail closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handler responds inside 10 seconds">
    Acknowledge, then process on a queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processing is deduplicated on sync_id + event">
    Up to 4 retries within 60 seconds means duplicates are guaranteed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production webhooks are registered - not just Development">
    They are environment-scoped and do not cross over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A scheduled reconciliation sweep exists">
    Retries stop after ~60 seconds. Anything longer than a one-minute outage
    loses events permanently, with no error anywhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reliability

<Steps>
  <Step title="429 is retried using Retry-After">
    200 requests per minute per connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="5xx is retried with capped exponential backoff">
    4xx other than 429 is never retried.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Concurrency across connectors is bounded">
    Rate limits are per connector, so Bindbee won't stop you - your own
    infrastructure will.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No resync on a timer">
    Only on explicit user intent or after a write. Frequent resyncs can get the
    connector throttled upstream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timeouts are set on every request">
    30 seconds is comfortable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Observability

<Steps>
  <Step title="integration_slug is on every log line and error">
    It converts "some customers fail" into "Workday customers fail".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connection health is polled and surfaced">
    `status` and `sync_status` for every connector. See
    [Monitor sync status](/how-to/troubleshooting/monitor-sync-status).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alerting fires on consecutive failures, not single ones">
    Transient vendor errors are routine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staleness alerting exists">
    A connector that quietly stops syncing serves plausible data indefinitely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sharp drops in total_employees gate deletion logic">
    Mirroring a bad sync's absent records as terminations is expensive to undo.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your customers see

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data age is displayed">
    "Last synced 4 hours ago". Highest-leverage item on this list for support
    volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="RELINK_NEEDED is prominent and actionable">
    The only state that never self-heals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First-sync state is distinct from empty">
    "Importing your data…" not an empty table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unsupported features degrade rather than error">
    Model and write support vary by connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual sync is throttled">
    Disabled while syncing, with a cooldown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security & compliance

<Steps>
  <Step title="You read only the models you need">
    `ssn`, `BankInfo` and compensation materially expand your obligations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access control exists inside your product">
    Bindbee authenticates your service, not your end users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Offboarding deletes the connector and your copy">
    `DELETE /api/{category}/v1/connectors/{id}/delete`, plus your retention
    policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trust Center is your answer to security reviews">
    [trust.bindbee.dev](https://trust.bindbee.dev) - link it rather than copying
    claims that go stale.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Final smoke test in Production

1. Connect one real connector via your production flow.
2. Confirm `origin_id` maps to the right tenant.
3. Wait for the first sync - confirm your UI shows progress, not an empty state.
4. Confirm `connector_synced` reaches your endpoint and passes signature verification.
5. Run a full import; confirm the count matches `total_employees`.
6. Run an incremental import; confirm the watermark advances.
7. Simulate a failure - return `500` from your webhook endpoint and confirm retries then your sweep recover.
8. Trigger a resync from your UI; confirm throttling works.
9. Confirm no credential appears in logs.

<Tip>
  Item 7 is the one teams skip and the one that matters most. The retry gap is
  narrow, and the sweep is the only thing that closes it.
</Tip>
