title: "Rename or Delete a Connector"
description: "Change a connector's display name, or remove one permanently - and understand what deletion destroys."
---

Renaming is cosmetic. Deletion is permanent and destroys the connector's record identities, which breaks every Bindbee ID your system has stored for that customer.

<Warning>
  Deletion is not a reset. To recover a broken connector, [force a resync](/how-to/sync-control/force-a-resync); to fix expired credentials, [relink it](/how-to/connections/update-credentials). Both preserve the connector and its IDs.
</Warning>

## Rename a connector

The display name identifies the connection in the dashboard and in webhook payloads as `connector.display_name`. It has no effect on the API - nothing is keyed on it.

Open the connector and edit the name from its page.

<Tip>
  Name connectors after the end customer rather than the integration. Once an account has several connectors on the same platform, a list of identically-named entries is unusable - and `origin_id`, set at Magic Link creation, is what actually resolves a connector back to your own customer record.
</Tip>

## Delete a connector

<Info>
  **Before you start**

  - You've confirmed deletion is what you want, not a resync or a relink.
  - Nothing downstream still references this connector's record IDs.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm nothing depends on the stored IDs">
    Deleting discards the connector's synced data and the record identities built from it. Reconnecting the same customer afterwards produces a **new** connector with new IDs - every employee, employment, and benefit ID you've stored becomes a dangling reference.

    **Result:** You know the blast radius.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Delete it">
    ```bash
    curl --request DELETE \
      --url 'https://api.bindbee.dev/api/v1/connectors/<CONNECTOR_ID>' \
      --header 'Authorization: Bearer <BINDBEE_API_KEY>'
    ```

    See [Delete Connector](/api-reference/connectors/delete-connector) for the exact request shape.

    From the dashboard, the control is on the connector's page.

    **Result:** The connector is removed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm your connector count">
    In Production, connectors are counted and billed, so deletion is sometimes the point. Filter to **Active Connectors** when checking - incomplete connectors persist and are routinely mistaken for ones that weren't deleted.

    **Result:** The count reflects what you expect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When deletion is the right call

| Situation | Delete? |
| --- | --- |
| An incomplete connector the end user abandoned | Yes |
| A duplicate created by mistake, never used | Yes |
| A customer that has genuinely churned | Yes |
| A test connection in Production that's being billed | No - [move it to Development](/how-to/setup/move-a-connector-between-environments) |
| Credentials expired | No - [relink](/how-to/connections/update-credentials) |
| Sync failing or data looks wrong | No - [resync](/how-to/sync-control/force-a-resync) |
| A long-running sync you want to interrupt | No - wait, then resync |

## If this didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You deleted a connector that was still in use">
    Reconnecting produces a new connector with new record IDs. Re-map your stored references to the new IDs, keying on a stable upstream identifier such as employee number or work email to match old records to new - accepting that neither is unique on every system.

    Contact support before doing this at scale.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connectors you deleted still appear">
    Check the Connectors tab filter. Incomplete connectors show alongside active ones and are the usual explanation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You want to stop a connector syncing without deleting it">
    Moving it to Development stops automatic syncs while preserving the connection and its history, and Development connectors aren't billed. See [Move a Connector Between Environments](/how-to/setup/move-a-connector-between-environments).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
