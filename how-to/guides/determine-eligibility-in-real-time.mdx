## Constraints specific to this combination

**"Real time" is the sync cadence.** Reads serve synced data, not a live query against the HRIS. On a 24-hour cadence, an eligibility answer can be a day old, and nothing in the response indicates that. If a real-time answer is a product commitment, either shorten the cadence - see [Change Sync Frequency](/how-to/sync-control/change-sync-frequency) - or force a resync before answering, accepting the latency and the load that adds.

**Stale answers are worse here than anywhere else.** A broken connector keeps returning the last successful sync, so an eligibility check on a stalled connection confidently reports a terminated employee as active. Subscribe to sync error, mark affected connections degraded, and return "unknown" rather than a stale answer. Returning nothing is recoverable; returning a wrong yes is not.

**Employment status alone is not eligibility.** Eligibility depends on waiting periods, hours thresholds, and benefit class rules held in the customer's benefits configuration, none of which are readable. An active employee may be ineligible; an employee on leave may retain eligibility. Compute against rules agreed per customer.

**Timestamp every answer.** Record and expose when the underlying data was last synced. It converts an unqualified assertion into a qualified one, and it is what makes a disputed answer diagnosable afterwards.

**Enrolment lags election.** A benefit elected during open enrolment may not be effective yet. Check the benefit's effective dates rather than treating any enrolment record as current coverage - see [Read Benefits Data](/how-to/reading-data/read-benefits-data).
