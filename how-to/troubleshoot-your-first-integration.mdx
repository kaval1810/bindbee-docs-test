These six account for most of what goes wrong while working through the
[tutorial](/get-started/tutorial-overview). Each lesson also ends with the
failures that lesson can cause - this page collects them in one place so you can
search by symptom.

| Symptom | Cause | Where it's fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `401 Unauthorized` | Missing the `Bearer ` prefix, or a key from the other environment | [Lesson 1](/get-started/tutorials/set-up-your-environment) |
| `404 Not Found` on a connector endpoint | Connector paths are category-scoped - `/api/hris/v1/connectors`, not `/api/v1/connectors` | [Lesson 1](/get-started/tutorials/set-up-your-environment) |
| `403` on `/employees` while `/connectors` works | The `X-Connector-Token` header is missing | [Lesson 3](/get-started/tutorials/connect-a-test-account) |
| `items` is `[]` and nothing is obviously wrong | Development connectors never sync on their own | [Lesson 4](/get-started/tutorials/run-your-first-sync) |
| A field you expected is `null` | Out of scope, not permitted by the source system, or not carried by the unified model | [How Bindbee decides what data you get](/explanation/what-data-you-get) |
| `429 Too Many Requests` | Per-connector rate limit exceeded | [Rate limits](/api-reference/basics/rate-limits) |

<Tip>
  For errors outside the tutorial - retry behaviour, error shapes, and what each
  status means in production - see [Handle errors](/how-to/handle-errors).
</Tip>
