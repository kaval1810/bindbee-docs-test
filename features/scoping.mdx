---
title: "Scoping"
description: "The list of models and fields Bindbee requests from a connected system - configured once for your organization and applied to every connector."
---

Scope is the list of models and fields Bindbee requests from a connected system. You set it once for your organization, under **Configure → Scoping** in the Dashboard, and it applies to every connector you create afterwards.

A field outside scope is never requested, so it never syncs and is absent from responses - not `null`, simply not there. Narrowing scope also narrows what your customer's admin has to grant access to.

<Info>
  Scope is **shared across environments**. What you configure in Development
  applies in Production too.
</Info>

<Tip>
  See [Scope the data you need](/get-started/tutorials/scope-your-data) for a
  step-by-step walkthrough of setting it up.
</Tip>
