New to Bindbee? Start with [What is Bindbee?](/get-started/what-is-bindbee) first.

Four things carry every integration, and they happen in that order. You set
**scope** once - the models and fields Bindbee may request. Your customer
authorizes their system, which creates a **connector**. That connector **syncs**,
pulling the scoped data and normalizing it. When something looks wrong, **logs**
show the exact request and response behind it.

The Dashboard is where you watch all four.
