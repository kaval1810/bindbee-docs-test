---
title: "Connectors"
description: "A connector is one customer's authorized link to one third-party system - where you find its status, issues, and logs."
---

A connector is the bridge between your application and a third-party tool. Each one is specific to a single integration and carries the credentials and state needed to move data between the two - in other words, a unique link between your product and the tool one of your customers uses.

<img
  src="https://images.unifyx.dev/documentation_images/connector.png"
  alt="The Connectors list in the Bindbee Dashboard"
  className="block"
/>

<Tip>
  From a connector you can also **track issues** and open its **logs** - both are
  the fastest route to why a sync misbehaved.
</Tip>

See [Core concepts](/explanation/core-concepts) for how connectors relate to environments, syncs, and connector tokens.
