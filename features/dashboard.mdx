---
title: "Dashboard"
description: "The central place to see how your integrations are performing: API volume, connector health, and which customer is on which system."
---

The Dashboard is where you manage your Bindbee integrations from one place. It gives you an overview of how they are performing: how many API requests have been made, which of your customers is on which connector, and whether any connector currently has a problem.

From here you can also reach the logs and the list of open connector issues.
